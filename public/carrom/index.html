Aim the striker, cover the queen.

Carrom for your phone, in English and Hindi. Play Classic, Freestyle or Disc Pool against the computer, or pass the phone to a friend. It plays offline.

At the carrom board

  • Three modes: Classic (cover the queen), Freestyle to 120 points, and Disc Pool
  • Play the computer at Easy, Medium or Hard, or two players on one phone with Pass and Play
  • Aaj ka Shot: a new carrom puzzle every day
  • Seven boards, from Royal Wood to Diwali Night. Looks only: every board plays the same.
  • A daily gift, a daily spin, goals, chests, streaks and trophies
  • Optional match commentary, spoken by your phone’s own voice
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose, and a banner on the home screen and during matches. No full-screen ads.
  • Plays offline, and ads need the internet. English and Hindi.

Coins are earned only, for play, and have no money value. They cannot be bought or cashed out. Package name: com.bharatjoy.carrom.
