Privacy policy
for Bharat Carrom

भारत कैरम · गोपनीयता नीति

Developer: Bharat Joy. This page is for the Android edition of Bharat Carrom on Google Play (package com.bharatjoy.carrom). Effective date: 24 November 2026. A Hindi version of this policy will follow.

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following in private storage on the device. It is not uploaded.

  • Saved matches, stats, rewards, streaks and unlocked items
  • Coin balance
  • Game settings, including the optional commentary voice
  • Ad limit and clock records (when you last watched a rewarded ad, and the last verified time), so the daily limit works

Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data deletes this local data. There is no Bharat Joy account, no sign-in and no game server, so there is nothing of yours to delete on our side. See the data deletion page for ad data and advertising ID reset or deletion steps.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Bharat Carrom shows Google AdMob ads in two formats only: rewarded video ads and banner ads. A rewarded ad never plays by itself: it starts only after you tap a button that shows an ad icon and names the reward, and there is a daily limit. A banner is shown on the home screen and during matches, including Pass and Play matches. There are no interstitial ads or other full-screen ads between games.

AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from the IP address, ad interactions, and its own diagnostics, to serve, personalise, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. Ads can be personalised using the advertising ID, depending on your consent choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, then Ads).

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before it requests ads, and your choice decides whether ads can be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, then Privacy choices. This entry appears when Google's consent rules apply to you.

AdMob is the only third party that receives data from this app. Bharat Carrom does not include Firebase or any other analytics service. Bharat Joy does not collect personal data or run servers for this game.

No name, picture or gender

Bharat Carrom does not ask for your name, a picture or your gender.

Verified time check

To stop daily rewards and ad limits being reset by changing the phone's clock, the Android app sends an HTTPS HEAD request, with no body, to https://www.google.com/generate_204 and reads only the Date header of the reply. The request carries no game data, no player details and no advertising or device ID. Like any web request, it lets Google see the phone's IP address. If the phone is offline, the app uses its own clock checks.

No reminders

Bharat Carrom does not schedule or show notifications.

Optional commentary voice

The optional commentary voice uses your phone's on-device text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded or sent from the phone for commentary. The app does not use the microphone or ask for microphone permission.

On-device features

Vibration only runs when that setting is on. The Privacy policy and Data deletion links in Settings open this website in your browser.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your name, picture, gender or coin balance.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show fake online players.
  • Coins are virtual play coins with no money value. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

Security

Everything the app sends over the internet, as described above, travels encrypted over HTTPS.

Age

Bharat Carrom is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a later version changes the ad network or analytics, or adds an account server, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before that version ships.
