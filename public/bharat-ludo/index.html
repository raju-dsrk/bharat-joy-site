Bharat Ludo

भारत लूडो

Closed testing on Google Play

Roll the dice, reach home.

An offline Ludo board in English and Hindi. Play vs AI, pass the phone with friends, or practise against the computer. The die is always fair.

On the board

  • Play vs AI, or practise against the computer
  • Pass and Play for 2, 3 or 4 players, always free
  • Quick Ludo for a shorter match
  • Fair, random dice for every player
  • One re-roll per person each match, from a short ad you choose. Computer players never re-roll.
  • Daily reward, daily spin (odds shown in the game), goals and coins
  • Festive and classic themes, and avatars
  • English by default, Hindi one tap away
  • Plays offline. Ads need the internet.

Coins are earned only, for play, and have no money value. They cannot be bought or cashed out. Package name: com.bharatjoy.ludo.
