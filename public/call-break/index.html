Make your call, then win your tricks.

The card game of calls and tricks, in English and Hindi. Thirteen cards each, five rounds, and computer players at three levels.

At the table

  • Classic Call Break: four players, 52 cards, and spades are always trump
  • Play three computer players at Easy, Medium or Hard, over five rounds
  • Pass and Play for 2 to 4 people on one phone, with computer players in empty seats
  • A free Practice table, and house rules you can change
  • Hints during play, and quick chat lines like Namaste and Well played
  • A daily reward, a daily spin, chests, missions, levels and a Festival Reward
  • Tables and card backs to unlock. Looks only: rules, deals and computer players stay the same.
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose, and a banner at the bottom of the game screen during a match. No full-screen ads.
  • English and Hindi

Coins are earned only, for play, and have no money value. They cannot be bought or cashed out. Package name: com.bharatjoy.callbreak.
