How to remove data from Bharat Joy games on Android. Effective date: 12 November 2026.

There is no account to delete

Our games do not have sign-in, cloud saves or a game server. Your name, picture, optional gender, coins and progress are kept on your phone (see the notes below for usage events and Android backup). This page covers:

  • Bharat Ludo (भारत लूडो), package com.bharatjoy.ludo
  • Bharat Block Puzzle (भारत ब्लॉक पहेली), package com.bharatjoy.blockpuzzle
  • Bharat Sweet Mela (भारत स्वीट मेला), package com.bharatjoy.sweets
  • Bharat Carrom (भारत कैरम), package com.bharatjoy.carrom
  • Bharat Sudoku (भारत सुडोकू), package com.bharatjoy.sudoku
  • Bharat Call Break (भारत कॉल ब्रेक), package com.bharatjoy.callbreak
  • Bharat Bubble Shooter (भारत बबल शूटर), package com.bharatjoy.bubbleshooter

Delete everything the game saved

Do either of these on your phone:

  1. Uninstall the game. This removes all of its saved data.
  2. Or open Settings, then Apps, choose the game, then Storage, and tap Clear storage (on some phones, Clear data). The game starts fresh next time.

We cannot restore this data after it is removed, because we never had a copy.

Advertising ID

Ads come from Google AdMob, which uses your phone's advertising ID. To delete or reset it, open Settings, then Google, then Ads, and choose Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID on older Android versions). Google handles ad data under its own privacy policy.

Usage events in Bharat Ludo, Bharat Block Puzzle

Bharat Ludo, Bharat Block Puzzle send usage data to Google Firebase Analytics with an app-instance ID and the advertising ID, never your name, picture or coin balance. Each game's privacy policy lists what is sent. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes the app-instance ID from your phone, so older events are no longer linked to it, and you can delete or reset the advertising ID as above. Google keeps those events only for the retention period set in our Firebase project, and then deletes them. If you would like us to ask for them to be deleted sooner, write to us.

Android backup in Bharat Call Break

Bharat Call Break allows Android's own backup. If backup is turned on for your Google account, a copy of the game's saved data may be kept in your phone's Google backup, even after you uninstall the game, until you turn backup off or delete that backup. To remove it, open Settings, then Google, then Backup on your phone (the path varies by phone), turn backup off or delete the backup, or manage backups in your Google account. Bharat Joy never receives this backup.
