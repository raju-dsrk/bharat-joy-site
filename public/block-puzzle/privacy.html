Privacy policy
for Bharat Block Puzzle

भारत ब्लॉक पहेली · गोपनीयता नीति

Developer: Bharat Joy. This page is for the Android edition of Bharat Block Puzzle on Google Play (package com.bharatjoy.blockpuzzle). Effective date: 12 November 2026. A Hindi version of this policy will follow.

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following in private storage on the device. It is not uploaded.

  • The name and icon you choose
  • Optional gender, if you choose to give it. It is never sent to ads, and you can clear it in Settings.
  • Coins, levels, stars, streaks, goals and records
  • Sound, vibration, reminder and language settings

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. There is no Bharat Joy account and no game server, so there is nothing of yours to delete on our side. See the data deletion page.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Bharat Block Puzzle shows Google AdMob ads in two formats only. A rewarded video is always your choice, for example to continue a game, double coins, get one extra spin a day, or use a helper (power-up): rotate, swap tray or undo. A banner is shown on the game screen only. There are no full-screen ads between games.

AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from the IP address, ad interactions, and its own diagnostics, to serve, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

AdMob is the only third party that receives data from this app. Bharat Block Puzzle does not include Firebase or any other analytics service.

Reminders

The evening reminder is optional and off until you turn it on. You can turn it on in Settings, or by allowing notifications when the app asks once after your first level win. A phone that allows notifications without asking does not turn it on. While it is on and notifications are allowed, the app schedules at most one local reminder a day, around 7 pm phone time, and none on a day you have already played.

The reminder is made and shown on this phone using Android's notification system. No data is sent for it and no push server is used. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, which cancels the pending reminder, or in your phone's settings.

Daily rewards and the clock

Daily rewards use the phone's own clock, with checks on the phone that stop a reward being claimed twice when the clock is moved. The app does not contact a time service.

Share card and vibration

The share card is a picture made on the phone. It holds your chosen name (or none) and your score, never your gender or icon. It leaves the phone only when you tap Share, through your phone's share sheet. The app does not receive the list of people you share with. Vibration only runs when that setting is on.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your name, picture, gender or coin balance.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show fake online players.
  • Coins are virtual play coins with no money value. They cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

Security

Everything the app sends over the internet, as described above, travels encrypted over HTTPS.

Children

Bharat Block Puzzle is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a later version changes the ad network or analytics, or adds an account server, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before that version ships.
