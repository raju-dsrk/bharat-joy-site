Bharat Sudoku

भारत सुडोकू

Coming soon

Fill the grid, one number at a time.

Classic Sudoku in English and Hindi, with the digits 1 to 9. Four levels from Easy to Expert, and the puzzles play offline.

In the puzzle

  • Classic 9x9 Sudoku: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
  • 300 levels on a level map, up to three stars each, plus Quick Play
  • A Daily Puzzle and a Weekly Mela of seven puzzles
  • Notes, undo, erase and hints. The first three hints are free.
  • Highlight same numbers, show conflicts, auto-remove notes and a focus mode
  • A daily reward, a spin, achievements, and board themes and number fonts you earn with coins
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose, and a banner at the bottom of the puzzle screen. No full-screen ads.
  • Festive and Classic looks. English and Hindi.

Coins are earned only, for play, and have no money value. They cannot be bought or cashed out. Package name: com.bharatjoy.sudoku.
