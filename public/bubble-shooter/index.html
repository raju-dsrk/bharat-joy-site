Aim, match three, pop.

Shoot from the lotus, match three or more of a colour and watch them pop. In English and Hindi, and it plays offline.

Level by level

  • 300 levels across valleys on a level map, up to three stars each
  • Level goals: clear the ceiling, free the parrots, light the diyas, beat the clock and rescue the peacock
  • Endless mode and a weekly Festival Level
  • Boosters: Fireball, Rainbow, +5 shots and Long Aim, for coins you earn
  • A daily gift, a streak with a weekly streak saver, daily and weekly goals, a weekly chest and achievements
  • Festive and Classic looks, and a share card
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose, and a banner on the level map and the game screen. No full-screen ads.
  • English and Hindi

Coins are earned only, for play, and have no money value. They cannot be bought or cashed out. Package name: com.bharatjoy.bubbleshooter.
