Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes!

Classic snakes and ladders in Hindi and English. Roll the die, climb the ladders, and land exactly on 100 to finish first.

On the board

  • Play one or three computer players, or Pass and Play for 2 to 4 friends on one phone
  • Shield and Re-roll cards in every match: a shield stops a snake, a re-roll tries that move again
  • Eleven boards from easy to hard, opened as you level up or with coins
  • A Festival Board that changes every week
  • Eight pawns to pick from, and your own name and icon
  • A daily reward, a daily spin and a chest
  • Rewarded ads only when you choose, and a banner on the game screen. No full-screen ads.
  • English and Hindi

Coins are earned only, for play, and have no money value. They cannot be bought or cashed out. Package name: com.bharatjoy.saanpseedhi.
